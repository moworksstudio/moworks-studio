# Brand and guideline (the Assets step, before any storyboard)

From the Moworks Motion kit 2.0.4 (`SKILL.md` step 3, `brands/other.md`). Brand packs kept by the app come later; until then a
guideline is a file the owner gives (in `sources/`, read through the pages the app prepared) or a link in the brief.

## 1. What a guideline governs

A guideline governs the DESIGN of **every piece of text and graphic** in the film, not only the main layout: fonts, type sizes, colours,
logo use and clear space, grid and margins, imagery, do / don't. In motion it binds only its logo and end-card rules; all other motion is
free, text effects included (glitch, stretch, blur, split…) while the text stays readable. Write its design rules in `NOTES.md` (fonts,
sizes, palette with hex, grid and margins, logo use, do / don't) and follow them on every frame.

**The design is set part by part**. New project's Brand Kit field (one of HyperFrames' frame presets or the owner's own Brand Kit) is
"From the brief" unless the owner chose one. A chosen Brand Kit wins for what it sets (colours, type, its do's and don'ts): leave the
brief's design words for those parts aside; a brief that asks otherwise is a conflict: stop and ask (moworks-studio §2b). The app writes
the Brand Kit to `frame.md` when the project is made; never write or change that file. A Brand Kit chosen and a guideline in the assets
that differs from it: a conflict, stop and ask (moworks-studio §2b). No Brand Kit: a guideline in the assets is followed, and your reply
suggests the owner save it as a Brand Kit in the app (to choose it next time). What the Brand Kit or guideline leaves open follows the
design the brief describes; what that leaves open is yours, as HyperFrames' `look.md` says (its frame presets are slide-deck looks: one
only when the owner names it, never the fallback for a film without a brand).

## 2. Asking

| Case | Do |
|---|---|
| A guideline in the assets or a link to one | read ALL of it (every page, not only its text), write its rules in `NOTES.md`; with no Brand Kit chosen, follow them; with a Brand Kit that differs: a conflict, stop and ask which wins for each point (moworks-studio §2b) |
| A client's brand, no guideline in the assets | no question (the owner prefers none): free design with the client's logo and colours from their material; a guideline added later is followed from then on |
| A personal idea, no brand | no question; design at maximum creative effort |
| The brief asks for something the guideline does not allow (colours above all, then fonts, sizes, capitals, logo use) | before the storyboard, list every point and ask the owner, one list, brief or guideline for each |
| Guideline and artwork disagree | a conflict: stop and ask (moworks-studio §2b), keep design suggesting the artwork first |

Free design without a guideline: say in `NOTES.md` which colours are the client's and which are yours ("own choice"); never present your
own colours as the brand's.

## 3. Fonts

The client's own font files (in `assets/1_design/fonts/`). Missing → ask for the file. Every font must draw every Vietnamese letter the film writes
(the app checks; HyperFrames' built-in fonts are latin-only). Otherwise the app's bundled Vietnamese-complete fonts.

## 4. Logo and end card

Official file only, original colours, never redrawn, stretched or given an effect (glitch, blur, split); it may enter and leave as a whole
(fade, slide, uniform scale) unless the guideline says otherwise; a logo animation the guideline itself gives is fine. Some brands never let
their logos move: follow the guideline. An end card with the logo on a background it reads on. Mid-film logo use only where the design has it
or the owner asks. A logo wall of the client's own clients (in their deck) keeps every logo as it is, each under the same rule.

## 5. Never on another brand's film

Another brand's end card, colours or product artwork as the hero; their rules do not apply.
