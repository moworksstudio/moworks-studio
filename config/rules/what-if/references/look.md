# A lit-room, stick-figure look (reference only)

Written from What If animation's `references/look.md` (FreshStack, MIT,
https://github.com/kelano86/what-if-animation/blob/3725a22d2d101320cae5b7d761af8d75120c4987/references/look.md), the look its author
measured on one kind of channel. **Only for a film whose style or brief asks for this look** (rooms in perspective, lit at dusk, simple
characters). Never apply it to motion graphics, line art, ink, Sumi-e, paper or any style with its own light and texture: the film
style's own look wins there.

- **Light from something you can see:** a window with the sun in it, a pendant, a wall light, a desk lamp, string lights; pools of light
  and dark corners, mid-dark walls so the lamps read.
- **A warm rim light** on the edge of each character that faces the light; the face the brightest thing in its area.
- **Depth:** a blurred, dark foreground in about a third of the shots; soft backgrounds on close-ups; haze or dust in the beams.
- **A full world:** a wide shows many designed objects; everything that stands has legs and a contact shadow; objects sit on their
  surfaces at the character's scale, toward the room's vanishing point.
- **Little text:** the title card and small readouts only (a timer, a label naming the power); no speech bubbles, no captions of the voice.
- **Characters** mostly in three-quarter or profile; holds near-still with small hand changes; expressions swap through a blink; the life
  comes from around them (a flash on an impact, dust, steam, a swaying lamp, a slow push-in).
