In Italy, one bank will lend you money if you bring it a giant wheel of cheese. Imagine you make Parmesan. Every wheel has to sit on a shelf for at least a year before you can sell it, but your bills are due now. So you roll your best wheel into a marble bank. A man in a suit taps it with a little steel hammer and listens for air bubbles inside. He nods, and a huge vault door swings open on shelves of cheese all the way to the ceiling, hundreds of thousands of wheels. Every day, people walk the aisles, checking them for cracks. Your friends think it's a joke. A bank full of cheese? You see, this cheese gets more valuable the longer it ages. The bank keeps your wheels in its vault and ages them for you. And if you can't pay the loan back, it simply sells the cheese and gets its money back. Finally, a loan backed by something that gets better with age.
