# The Bank Vault Full of Cheese: fact check (2026-10-26)

The bank is **Credem (Credito Emiliano)**, Reggio Emilia. The voice says "one bank" / "an Italian bank" only; no bank
name, logo or branding appears on screen (the set's sign is a generic "BANCA").
