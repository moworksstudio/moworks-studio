# How these shorts are written

There is no narrator voice. The story is carried by **pictures, a few white captions (the narrator) and red typed
words (the inner voice, the shadow)**. A 2–3 minute film has maybe 15–25 lines in total.

## The shape of a full short
The classic arc of a 2–3 minute short in this format, part by part.

| # | Part | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Cold open** | One white sentence on black, establishing a routine ("every day was the same" type). About 3 s. |
| 2 | **The routine** | Bedroom at night. The alarm. The inner voice bargains in red and the clock jumps to show the cost. A one-word red verdict. |
| 3 | **The pattern** | `DAY 1`: the black shadow figure appears beside him and gives advice in red. More `DAY` scenes with **big jumps** in the number (tens, then hundreds) show the same defeat in new rooms: the desk, the fridge, a mirror where the shadow is the reflection. |
| 4 | **The low point** | A short white line of surrender. He is alone, crouched in a pool of light. Fade to near-black. |
| 5 | **The turn** | Silence. He sits on the edge of the bed. A decision with no words. |
| 6 | **The rebuild** | The DAY counter **restarts low and climbs** (single digits → tens → hundreds): night runs in a red headband, push-ups with a counter, a punching bag. The shadow still shouts, but now it's fuel. The alarm rings and he's already up. |
| 7 | **Climax** | Stairs and then a summit at sunset; the palette turns warm for the first time. A white line about what kept him going. |
| 8 | **Coda** | The bedroom again at dawn. The shadow is small in the corner. |
| 9 | **Title card** | One word in big Comic Sans with a **red underline**, then a closing white line and a red two-word payoff. |

## The formula (for an original short)
1. **Theme:** one abstract noun for the title (the habit or virtue). It's shown only at the end.
2. **The want:** one concrete, drawable goal, such as an instrument, a run, a book or a sketchbook. The object should
   be visible in the room from the first scene.
3. **The voice:** the shadow is the excuse given a body. Every red line is something people really tell themselves:
   comfort, delay, insult, doubt.
4. **Two counters:**
   - **Act 1 (failure):** the `DAY` numbers jump far apart, so time passes and nothing changes.
   - **Act 2 (work):** the count restarts and climbs, so time passes and something changes.
5. **The arc in pictures:**
   - **The shadow:** big and close while it wins, then small and behind him once he grows.
   - **The light:** cold blue phone light and moonlight first, warm light only at the end.
   - **The character:** heavy lids and slumps first, then a headband and gritted teeth.
6. **The silence beat:** the low point and the turn have no words, and the sound drops to the room tone.
7. **The ending:** the title word with a red underline and one closing line that hands the choice to the viewer.

## Line rules
| | White (narrator) | Red (inner voice / shadow) |
|---|---|---|
| Person and tense | 3rd person, past ("He bought…") | 2nd person or imperative, present ("put it down.") |
| Length | ≤ 7 words, one per screen | ≤ 4 words, lower case, ending "." or "…" |
| Motion | fades in whole, holds 2–3 s | types on (about 20 cps), holds about 1 s, fades |
| Place | centred, or high in the frame | by the speaker: above the sleeper, beside the shadow's head |
| How often | 1 per scene at most | 1–2 per temptation |

- Nothing should explain the moral before the end. The pictures show it.
- **No invented facts or statistics.** If a line states something real ("it takes 66 days…"), research it and source
  it, or cut it.
- When the user gives a reference video, don't reuse its lines, characters or shot-for-shot staging. Write new ones
  in the same grammar.

## Beat sheet template
Fill one in before building. Times are on the edit clock.

| t (s) | Picture | Words | Sound |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–3 | black | white: the routine in one line | silence, then room tone (music only if asked) |
| 3–13 | the routine room, static | white: the want; red: the excuse; time-skip gag; red verdict | foley: taps, clock |
| 13–14 | fade through black | | |
| 14–20 | `DAY 1`: the first try | red ×2 from the shadow | the try's sound; silence; low swell |
