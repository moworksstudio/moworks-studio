# Design System: Moworks Studio

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "One Shell, Two Worlds"**

Moworks Studio is a long-session production tool, laid out for a 4K screen at 150 %. One working shell (navigation, a top bar with status chips, panes with hairline splitters, galleries, dialogs) carries two themes chosen at first start and switchable in Settings › General › Appearance. **The Desk** (default) is deep water: a blue-black ground lit from the upper right by a soft radial depth, translucent chrome, a teal current, monospaced caps on buttons and small labels, hairline-thin condensed page titles. **The Studio** is an animation studio at night: a warm dark room around a lit light table, where everything the user reads or works on is paper, pegged to a peg bar, labelled by hand, ruled like a pad.

Both themes share the data, the components and every dialog's structure; a dialog keeps its boxes in the same places in both. The Desk keeps a fixed shell with a left rail; The Studio replaces the rail with a peg bar and lays out its outer pages ("rooms") as furniture. The chrome stays exact and quiet so the films, the library previews and Claude's work carry the screen. The Editor's centre is HyperFrames' own player, which is not part of this system.

The whole UI scales with the window. The root size is `calc(clamp(12.5px, min(9.5px + 0.215vw, 5.5px + 1.1vh), 15px) * var(--ui-scale))` (15px on the 2560 px work area, about 13.6px at 1920, 12.5px at 1366) and every size is in rem. The Text size setting multiplies it: Small 0.92, Medium 1, Large 1.1. The interface is English only; film content, briefs and names may be Vietnamese, so every face carries Vietnamese except the mono faces, which never set words.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Two themes. The Desk is the default and leaves `<html>` without a `data-theme` attribute (its own rules are written `:root:not([data-theme])`); The Studio sets `data-theme="studio"` and every one of its selectors starts with it, so The Desk keeps every pixel. Switching crossfades once (View Transition, 260 ms).
- Everything in rem off a window-scaled root; small floors keep text legible at the smallest root.
- Dark around the work in both themes. The Desk is tonal and hairline-built; The Studio puts light paper on a dark room.
- Caps only where the world speaks (page titles in The Desk, buttons, small section labels, table heads); navigation, tabs, chips and everything a person reads is sentence case.
- One signature per world: The Desk's depth gauge; The Studio's peg bar, light table and the film's three materials.
- Lucide line icons, scaled to rem; no glyph icons, no emoji.

## Colors

Each theme fills the same roles (a ground, stepped surfaces, two hairlines, three inks, one accent family, the status colours); components name roles (`--surface-2`, `--accent-ink`, `--state-done`), never a theme's hex.

### Primary
- **Current Teal** (The Desk `pelagra-teal`): filled primary actions with dark on-teal text, done nodes of the depth gauge, the active tab underline, splitters while dragged.
- **Registration Red** (The Studio `studio-registration`): the one colour that acts or approves: the primary action, the Create stamp, box numbers, the margin line on paper, the focus ring, the pinned sheet. Text on it is `studio-on-registration`.
- **Accent Ink** (`pelagra-teal-ink`; The Studio uses the **Lamp** `studio-lamp` on the room and `studio-registration-ink` on paper): the accent as text and icons (active rail icon, pressed segments and chips, links, gauge numerals, focus ring in The Desk). Filled accent is for surfaces; accent ink is for type.
- **Accent Soft / Line / Glow** (`pelagra-teal-soft` 13 %, `pelagra-teal-line` 45 %, `pelagra-teal-glow` 62 %): pressed chips, the current wizard step, "working" boxes, input focus halo; the glow is the light a primary action throws.

### Secondary
- **Second Tag** (`pelagra-tag-2`; The Studio `studio-pencil-light` on the room, `studio-pencil-ink` on paper): the style tag beside the format tag (which takes the accent), and the second light in the project card's surface.
- **The three materials** (The Studio): a film's progress is the material it is drawn in: **blue pencil** (`studio-pencil`, not started) → **ink** (`studio-ink`, in progress) → **colour** (a gradient `studio-colour-from` → `studio-colour-to`, rendered). Pictures take the material as a filter; the development bar under a film takes it as segments.

### Neutral
- **Ground** (`pelagra-ground` with the radial depth `radial-gradient(90rem 50rem at 72% -10%, pelagra-depth-glow, transparent 70%)`; `studio-room` with a faint lamp glow from the top centre).
- **Surfaces 1 / 2 / 3**: panes, cards, dialogs (1); hover fills, thumbnail beds, menus (2); menu-item hover, toasts, slates (3). The Studio's room surfaces are `studio-room-2`, `studio-room-3`, `studio-room-4`.
- **Hairline / Hairline Strong**: dividers and card borders; input, segmented and dialog borders. The Desk's buttons and input hover use the brighter `pelagra-control-line`; The Studio uses `studio-room-button-line` on the room and `studio-paper-edge` on paper.
- **Ink / Secondary / Muted**: primary text; supporting text and idle controls; hints, counts, timestamps. Muted stays above 4.5:1 on its ground.
- **Paper** (The Studio): `studio-paper-white` for sheets, dialogs, menus and toasts; `studio-paper` / `-2` / `-3` as paper's surfaces; `studio-lamp-paper` the lit centre of the light table; `studio-paper-edge` paper borders and scrollbars; `studio-paper-chip` a pressed paper tab; `studio-paper-line` dividers on paper; `studio-rule` and `studio-rule-tint` ruled lines. On paper, ink is `studio-ink`, `-2`, `-3`.
- **Room details** (The Studio): `studio-label-tape` / `studio-label-ink` / `studio-label-ink-2` (the label-maker search strip), `studio-plank-ink` / `-2` (words printed on a wooden shelf), `studio-tape` (masking tape), `studio-stop-on-room` (a missing folder on the room), `studio-room-hover` (a hover wash).
- **Editor scope** (`editor-*`, both themes): inside the Editor, surfaces, hairlines and the two grey inks become neutral greys, with `editor-well` behind the player; the accent remains only for the primary action, the playhead, the selection and the current stage.

### Status
Green `status-ok` = running, ready, connected, done, chosen; yellow `status-pending` = pending, downloading, in progress, waiting; red `status-stop` = stopped, missing, not connected, error. Grey (muted ink) only for "not needed". Each has a `-soft` fill, a `-line` border and an `-ink` for text on the full colour. Selection uses the green (`--select`). On The Studio's paper the same roles take darker inks (`studio-paper-done`, `studio-paper-run`, `studio-paper-stop`) so they hold contrast on light paper. Status reads by icon or text plus colour, never colour alone: top-bar dots, project status chips (in progress and needs review yellow, exported green, folder missing red), health checks, model and key rows, Claude's result pills.

### Fixed roles
Values that are not a theme's palette have a name too (`tokens.css`), and components use the name: **Media** (`media-bg` behind a picture, `on-media` over one, `media-label` under a mark on a picture, `media-overlay` behind a lightbox, `media-paper` the white page a motion preview is drawn on, `shade` shadows over pictures). **Grounds** (`well` recessed inputs and code; `fill-active` the chosen navigation item; `hairline-light` a light outline on dark media; `button-light` the top light inside a filled button). **Marks** (`ink-on-tag` text on an author tag; `ae-bg` / `ae-ink` the After Effects icon). **Timeline** (`tl-*`).

### Named Rules
**The Ink-Versus-Fill Rule.** Accent text uses accent ink; the raw accent is a fill, a 1-2px line, or a node. Text on a filled accent uses on-accent, never pure white.

**The Translucent Water Rule (The Desk).** The Desk's chrome lets the depth gradient through: top bar `rgba(6, 17, 26, 0.72)`; rail 0.6, panes 0.66, panels 0.7, cards 0.78 (all over `pelagra-surface-1`); the Editor's centre pane is solid ground.

**The One Red Rule (The Studio).** Registration red marks the one action or approval on a sheet. It is never a state colour; the small status dots keep green, yellow and red.

## Typography

**The Desk:** Saira Condensed Light for display, Be Vietnam Pro for body and interface, M PLUS 1 Code for labels and code, Agdasima for numerals.
**The Studio:** Fraunces for page and sheet titles, Bricolage Grotesque for the interface, DM Mono for numbers and small capital labels, Patrick Hand for handwritten notes.

**Character:** The Desk sets slender, thin condensed caps over a quiet light humanist sans, with tracked mono caps for labels. The Studio sets a soft editorial serif over a lively grotesque, with mono labels like a label maker and a handwriting face for what a person would pencil in. All faces are bundled WOFF2, credited in `ui/src/fonts/README.md` and `ui/src/fonts/studio/README.md`; nothing is fetched at run time.

### Hierarchy (The Desk)
- **Display** (page titles, h1): 300, 2.75rem / 3rem, +0.03em, uppercase, balanced wrap.
- **Title** (section titles, empty states, h2): the display face at 1.375rem / 1.75rem, sentence case.
- **Heading** (card titles, dialog titles, h3): Be Vietnam Pro at the strong weight, 1.0625rem. Dialog titles use this, not the display face.
- **Body** (1rem / 1.55, 300, +0.0076em: the advance width of 400).
- **Nav** (rail items: 500, 0.9375rem / 1.3, sentence case).
- **Control** (max(0.875rem, 12px)): inputs, menus, banners, dense body.
- **Meta** (max(0.8125rem, 12px), 400): secondary lines, hints, timestamps, tags.
- **Dense** (max(0.75rem, 11px)): counts, shot meta, fine print.
- **Label** (max(0.8125rem, 11px), line box 1.125rem, uppercase): buttons at 0.1em; field labels, panel and pane heads, table heads and rail section labels at 0.2em.
- **UI text** (sentence case, the body face at the strong weight, no tracking): tabs, segmented buttons, chips, top-bar status chips, pager buttons, Claude's status pills.
- **Numerals** (time codes, counts, gauge "02/05"): Agdasima at 1.15x, +0.08em, tabular where they align.
- **Code** (max(0.8125rem, 12px), 400): file names, step lists.

### Hierarchy (The Studio)
- **Room title** (`studio-display`): Fraunces 400, `clamp(2.6rem, 2rem + 2vw, 4.2rem)` / 0.95, -0.02em, sentence case, followed by a handwritten aside (Patrick Hand 1.25rem, tilted -2deg) on the same baseline row.
- **Sheet title** (`studio-sheet-title`): Fraunces 2.1rem on the light table, 2.6rem on a model sheet; dialog titles Fraunces 500.
- **Interface** (`studio-body`, `studio-ui`): Bricolage Grotesque 400 body, 500 for tabs and chips; strong weight 600.
- **Label** (`studio-label`): DM Mono 500 caps, max(0.72rem, 11px), 0.16em; buttons track at 0.04em.
- **Numerals** (`studio-num`): DM Mono at 1x, +0.02em.
- **Hand** (`studio-hand`, `studio-hand-ruled`): Patrick Hand for asides, the note under a film, a character's description, the drop zone's helper line and the reason Create is blocked; 1.45rem on the ruled line in the Brief.

### Named Rules
**The Floor Rule.** Meta, control and code text never fall under 12px; dense and label text never under 11px, whatever the root size or Text size setting. Every small size is written `max(…, 11px)` or `max(…, 12px)`.

**The Weight Token Rule.** Emphasis uses `--strong-weight` (500 The Desk, 600 The Studio), never a literal weight; labels use `--label-weight`.

**The Words-Not-In-Mono Rule (The Studio).** DM Mono carries no Vietnamese: it sets numbers and short capital labels only. Brand and author names are never set in Patrick Hand.

**The Capitals Rule.** Capitals belong to The Desk's page titles, buttons and small section labels (including the rail's section labels). Navigation items, tabs, chips, lists and the Settings section list stay sentence case.

## Layout

**The Desk.** A fixed shell grid: the rail (`max(14.5rem, 196px)`, collapsible to a 64px icon strip) and main, under a 46px top bar that spans both. Pages sit inside `page-max` (150rem) with `2rem 3rem 3rem` padding. A page head puts the display title and one line of secondary text on the left and actions on the right, then a toolbar (search, segmented filter, sort, view toggle), then content. Grids are auto-fill (film cards `minmax(min(20rem, 100%), 1fr)` at a 1rem gap). The library galleries are a three-part layout: a 15.5rem facet column, the grid, a 23rem inspector. Workspaces use resizable panes with 7px hit-area splitters drawn as 1px hairlines. Settings is a 15rem sticky nav plus a 62rem column of rows.

**The Studio.** No rail: a 3.5rem peg bar across the top, and below it a room that never scrolls; each screen lays out in what is left and only a long list scrolls inside its frame. Rooms share one head (room title and aside) on the same margins: `clamp(1rem, 2.2vh, 2.25rem) clamp(1.25rem, 3vw, 3.5rem) clamp(1rem, 2vh, 2rem)`. Under 1100px rooms stack to one column and may scroll.

**Shared.** Spacing follows the seven-step scale (0.25, 0.5, 0.75, 1, 1.5, 2, 3rem). Controls come in three heights: compact 2rem (chips, tabs, small buttons), standard 2.375rem (buttons, search, toolbar selects), large 2.75rem (form inputs, large buttons). Sizes already follow the window, so breakpoints change arrangement only:
- **1280px:** the pipeline goes to three columns; page padding steps down to `1.5rem 2rem 2rem`.
- **1100px:** The Desk's rail collapses to icons and Settings stacks its nav above the rows; The Studio's rooms go to one column.
- **900px:** page padding 1rem; two-up forms go single; list rows drop secondary columns.
- **760px:** the New project dialog becomes one column.
- **640px:** The Desk's rail becomes a 3.5rem bottom bar.

## Elevation & Depth

**The Desk** is flat at rest. Depth comes from tonal steps, 1px hairlines and the chrome's translucency over the radial depth. What floats above the page is **glass**: a slightly lighter blue tint (`pelagra-glass` for menus, popovers, lists and toasts; `pelagra-glass-thick` for dialogs; `pelagra-glass-sheet` for the Editor's reading sheets) over a backdrop blur (`blur(28px) saturate(175%)`, thinner layers `blur(20px) saturate(165%)`), a 1px light edge at 17 %, an inset top highlight and a soft sheen down the first rows. A list that opens inside a glass dialog is thicker, so text never sits on two translucent layers. Under reduced transparency or more contrast, every glass layer is solid surface 1.

**The Studio** is physical. Paper and objects cast soft, warm shadows because they are things lying on or standing in front of something: sleeves on a shelf, books, the pitch card taped to the wall, sheets laid on the light table. There is no glass: floating layers are paper (`studio-paper-white`) under a deep shadow with a warm lamp bloom; the light table glows from inside.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Glass** (The Desk `--glass-shadow`: `0 1.5rem 3.5rem -1.25rem rgba(0, 6, 12, 0.7), 0 0.25rem 0.75rem rgba(0, 6, 12, 0.35)` with the inset highlight `inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.18)`): dialogs, sheets, menus, popovers, lists, toasts.
- **Paper float** (The Studio: `0 1.5rem 3rem rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.45), 0 0 4rem rgba(255, 214, 150, 0.12)`): dialogs, menus, toasts, the Claude popover.
- **Light table** (The Studio: `0 0 0 1px rgba(255, 240, 210, 0.25), 0 0 4rem rgba(255, 214, 150, 0.16), 0 1.5rem 3rem rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.45)` over a radial `lamp-paper → paper → paper-2` fill): the table, a Settings panel, the Motion Library inspector.
- **In hand** (The Studio: `0 0 0 2px studio-lamp` plus a lamp bloom): the chosen sleeve, book, flipbook or film.
- **Current glow** (The Desk `--glow`: a 0.25rem teal ring at 24 % plus a 0.875rem bloom at 75 %; The Studio the same in lamp): the depth gauge's current node only.
- **Status glow**: a 0.5rem bloom in the dot's own status colour behind a coloured status dot.
- **Action glow** (The Desk): a primary action carries a faint accent glow (`0 0.25rem 1rem -0.5rem pelagra-teal-glow`) and brightens it on hover. The Studio's primary carries a red cast shadow (`0 0.4rem 1.2rem rgba(210, 60, 34, 0.32)`).
- **Selection ring** (`0 0 0 1px var(--select) inset` with a `--select` border): selected cards, theme cards, radio cards, aspect choices. The Studio's theme cards and chooser tiles ring in registration red.
- **Focus**: a 2px `--focus-ring` outline at 2px offset; inputs instead turn the border accent and add a 3px accent-soft halo.

### Named Rules
**The Float-Only Rule (The Desk).** A drop shadow means "this is above the page and will go away". Panels, panes and cards carry none at rest; selection is an inset ring, not a lift.

**The Light-Is-Water Rule (The Desk).** The Desk's light is luminous, not cast: glow on the live node, the live status and the primary action, never an offset shadow.

**The Things-Cast-Shadows Rule (The Studio).** A shadow belongs to an object that would cast one (paper, a sleeve, a book, a shelf, a strip of film). Flat interface parts on the room (tabs, chips, the label maker) carry none.

## Shapes

**The Desk:** soft, rounded corners on every box and popup: sm 0.375rem (small tags, code, thumbnails), md 0.625rem (menu items, rail items, media, slates), lg 0.875rem (banners, menus, toasts, result cards, theme cards, New project boxes), xl 1.125rem (panels, lists, dialogs, the drawer's open edge, empty states, the request bubble), card 1.375rem (cards: the media corner plus the 0.5rem inset, so the corners are concentric), ctl 0.625rem (buttons, inputs, segmented tracks; segments sit 0.1875rem inside), pill (chips, status chips, pager buttons, progress bars), xs 0.1875rem for tiny marks. Borders are 1px hairlines; buttons and gauge nodes use 1.5px. Thumbnails are 16:9 beds clipped to the card; a film with no rendered frame shows a **slate**: its title in the display face at heading size, uppercase, two lines at most, centred over a surface-3 to surface-2 gradient.

**The Studio:** tighter, paper-like corners: `studio-xs` 0.2rem (stamps, rules, stills), `studio-sm` 0.3rem (sleeves, chips, the label maker, fields on paper), `studio-md` 0.4rem (paper cards, the pitch paper, drop zones), `studio-lg` 0.6rem (sheets, Settings panels, the Create stamp), `studio-xl` 0.9rem (the light table, the film in hand). Paper tabs round only their top corners (0.5rem). Wood planks, paper edges, tape strips, peg holes and dashed pencil lines carry the rest of the form language; sleeves stand tilted (-7deg on a shelf) and straighten when taken.

### Named Rules
**The Ruled Paper Rule (The Studio).** Everything written on rules shares one line: `--st-line` (2.2rem), with the rule drawn 1px at 0.74 of it (`--st-rule-at`), just under the baseline of Patrick Hand at 1.3-1.45rem. Ruled paper is painted with `background-size: 100% var(--st-line)` and `background-origin: content-box`, and the text's line height is `--st-line`, so handwriting sits on the rules, never through them. The Brief and the Projects pitch card use it; a larger face on the same card (the pitch title) is lifted onto its rule. Fields that are not handwriting (selects, pickers, the title) are not ruled.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** write every size in rem off the window-scaled root and keep the 12px / 11px floors with `max()`.
- **Do** use role variables (`--surface-2`, `--accent-ink`, `--font-label`, `--r-ctl`, `--state-done`) in shared components; a theme is a block of variables plus its own scoped rules.
- **Do** scope every Studio rule under `[data-theme="studio"]` and every Desk-only rule under `:root:not([data-theme])`, so neither theme moves a pixel of the other.
- **Do** keep a shared dialog's boxes in the same places in both themes; a theme changes its colours, type and material only.
- **Do** put every caps label through the one label role and every sentence-case control through the UI-text role.
- **Do** keep one filled primary per area (one red stamp per sheet in The Studio).
- **Do** keep The Desk's shadows on floating layers only, and mark selection with the 1px inset ring.
- **Do** write handwriting on ruled paper with `--st-line` and `--st-ruled`, never a separate line height.
- **Do** guard hover styles with `(hover: hover) and (pointer: fine)` and keep transitions on the named curves.
- **Do** show a film's progress with the Gauge (The Desk) or the development bar (The Studio) wherever progress appears.

### Don't:
- **Don't** add capitals to navigation, tabs, chips or lists.
- **Don't** mark the active rail item with a coloured side stripe; fill and ink only.
- **Don't** put a drop shadow on a resting card, panel or pane in The Desk.
- **Don't** set pure white text on a filled accent; use on-accent.
- **Don't** use registration red as a state colour, or set words in DM Mono.
- **Don't** write literal font weights in components; use `--strong-weight` / `--label-weight`.
- **Don't** fetch fonts or images at run time, or add a face without its credit row in the fonts README.
- **Don't** show file paths, addresses or internal ids in the UI, or repeat a screen's title.
- **Don't** restyle the film inside the player; style the frame around it, never the picture.
- **Don't** use glyphs or emoji as icons; use lucide line icons at the rem-mapped sizes.

## The Studio

The Studio is the second theme, an animation studio at night. The app is a warm dark **room** around a lit **light table**; everything the user reads or works on is **paper** laid on it. A film's progress is its **material**: blue pencil (not started) → ink (in progress) → colour (rendered). **Registration red** marks the one action and the pinned sheet; the **lamp** is warmth, focus and Claude. It shares The Desk's components, data and dialog structure; it is switched by `<html data-theme="studio">` and lives in `ui/src/studio-shell.css` (tokens, shell, rooms) and `ui/src/studio-editor.css` (the Editor as a light table), loaded after every other stylesheet. Its fonts (SIL OFL) are in `ui/src/fonts/studio/`.

- **Tokens:** the `studio-*` colours, type roles and radii above are defined once in `studio-shell.css` (`--room`, `--paper`, `--pencil`, `--registration`, `--lamp`, `--st-r-*`, `--st-line`) and re-point The Desk's role variables, so every shared component takes the room's colours. Anything laid on the table (dialogs, menus, lists, toasts, Settings panels, `.studio-paper`) re-points them again to paper.
- **Contrast on paper:** words in pencil use `studio-pencil-ink` (6.1:1), field hints `studio-pencil-hint` (3.4:1, hints only), small red words `studio-registration-ink` (5.4:1). `studio-pencil`, `studio-pencil-light` and `studio-registration` stay for lines, dashes, stamps and fills.
- **Materials as filters:** a picture in pencil is greyscale, contrasted and tinted blue in multiply; in ink, greyscale and contrasted; in colour, untouched.
- **Shared rules that still hold:** the type floors, the weight tokens, sentence-case navigation, hover only on fine pointers, reduced motion turning movement off, lucide icons.
